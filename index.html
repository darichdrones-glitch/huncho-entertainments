ABOUT HUNCHO

HUNCHO ENTERTAINMENTS is an independent entertainment label representing music, creativity and talent from Atlantis, South Africa.

Our mission is to create opportunities for artists, develop new talent and build a platform where creativity can reach audiences beyond our community.
